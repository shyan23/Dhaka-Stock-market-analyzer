# 🚀 START HERE - Stock Market Analyzer

**Welcome! This is your personal Stock Market Analyzer.**

---

## 🎯 Quick Start (Choose One)

### Option 1: Use Local Database (Easiest)
**Just double-click:** `deploy-windows.bat`
✅ **No setup needed**
✅ **Works immediately**
✅ **Data stays on your computer**

### Option 2: Use Google Sheets (Advanced)
**First time setup:**
1. **Double-click:** `setup-google-sheets.bat`
2. **Follow the wizard**
3. **Then double-click:** `deploy-windows.bat`

✅ **Data syncs to Google Sheets**
✅ **Can share with others**
✅ **Automatic cloud backup**

---

## 📊 What You Need for Google Sheets

- **service.json file** (your developer will give you this)
- **Google Sheets ID** (from your spreadsheet URL)

**The setup wizard will guide you through everything!**

---

## 🌐 Using the App

1. **Start the app** by double-clicking `deploy-windows.bat`
2. **Open your browser** to: http://localhost:8501
3. **Add stocks** you want to track
4. **Record transactions** when you buy/sell
5. **View your portfolio** and profits

**Your data saves automatically!**

---

## 🛑 Stopping the App

**Run this command:**
```
.\deploy-windows.ps1 stop
```

**Or just close your browser** (the app keeps running in background)

---

## 🔄 Switching Between Storage Types

**You can switch anytime:**
1. **Open the app**: http://localhost:8501
2. **Go to Settings**
3. **Click "Storage Backend Switching"**
4. **Choose your preferred storage**
5. **Click "Migrate"**

**Your data transfers safely!**
